# 5.7 General rehearsal

Risk: medium. No money moves: every payment goes through the simulator in test mode. Sources: `docs/phases/phase-5.md` (criteria), `docs/done/5.1` to `5.6`, `docs/plans/5.1`, `5.3`.

## Design

- **One end-to-end spec**, `apps/web/e2e/rehearsal.spec.ts` (staff slot 26, one owner), walking the customer journey in order, with a staff browser and a customer browser:
  1. A published race with a priced package that is still `coming_soon`. The race page shows the alert form.
  2. Two addresses subscribe. Only one confirms, through the real confirm page and the token minted by the real mail send step.
  3. Staff opens the package. The opening notice goes to the confirmed address and not to the unconfirmed one (checked in the outbound email table).
  4. The confirmed address signs in as a customer, configures the package in the builder, sends the request, staff approves it.
  5. Deposit through checkout and the simulator, traveller details, balance through a single-use payment link, documents (confirmation, itinerary, voucher), as PDF files.
  6. Customer downloads her data export (no passport or medical keys), and a second customer cannot read the booking.
  7. Staff start page shows the alert subscriber count; the race list shows the confirmed count.
  8. At every page visited, no field asks for a passport or medical or dietary data.
- **Two small test-only scripts** in `apps/api/scripts/` (refuse outside `test` and `ci`, like the existing ones): `e2e-mail.ts` boots the API with a capturing mail sender and no listener, delivers the pending events and sends the queued emails to the addresses it is given, then prints what was sent. Nothing leaves the process.
- **Missing-to-open list** goes in `docs/done/5.7.md` (25 lines at most). It is built by reading the done notes, `docs/BLOCKED.md` and the parked items, not by guessing.

## Failure cases covered
Unconfirmed address gets no opening email; the customer of another booking sees nothing; a spent payment link is refused; a page that asks for passport or medical data fails the run.

## Not doing
Refunds, ledger, GST/FX, supplier payments, receipts, real keys or live mode. Balance reminders and expiry are time-based (covered by their own tests); not rehearsed here. Erasure is covered by 5.5's tests and needs a finished trip.

## Assumptions
The race page is cached for 60 seconds, so the spec reloads it until the package shows as open. Slot 26 is free on main (25 is another open task's).
